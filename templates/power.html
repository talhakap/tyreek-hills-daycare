How the rankings work

Every team gets a score from 0 to 100, built from three things:

  • All-play record ({{ (weights.get("all_play_weight", 0.40) * 100)|round|int }}%): your record if you'd played every other team every week. It shows how good a team really is, whatever the schedule.
  • Points for ({{ (weights.get("points_for_weight", 0.35) * 100)|round|int }}%): total points scored this season.
  • Recent form ({{ (weights.get("recent_form_weight", 0.25) * 100)|round|int }}%): average score over the last {{ weights.get("recent_form_weeks", 3) }} weeks, so hot and cold streaks count.

Each part is scaled so the best team that week gets 1 and the worst gets 0, then they're weighted and added up. ▲ and ▼ show movement since last week.

Luck is your real win % minus your all-play win %. Positive means you've won more than your scores deserved.
